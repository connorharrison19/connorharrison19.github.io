Case Studies

TELUS Expert Messaging

Content strategy for the first asynchronous messaging app in Canadian Telecom. Customers describe their issue and go about their day while a human expert resolves it. My work spanned IVR prompts, bot triage flows, agent training, marketing collateral, and future-state design.

What I solved

IVR drop-off → Switched to keyword-driven brevity, improving containment rate
Bot-to-expert handoff → Simplified handoff language to reduce cognitive load
Agent consistency → Built content snippets and a rubric to standardize first-message resolution
Content ecosystem → Owned hundreds of bilingual copy artifacts across the full dev handoff
$2.5M Projected annual call-offload savings
400K+ Conversations handled through messaging
6 min Reduction in average handling time vs. voice
+9 pts Lift in First Contact Resolution vs. voice
24/7 Support, with 30% of conversations outside business hours
5 pp Direct FCR increase from content strategy
